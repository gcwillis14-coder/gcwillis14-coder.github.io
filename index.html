Web App · Live Music & Maps

Giggity - London gigs

I kept hearing about gigs after they had happened or sold out. Giggity pulls listings from Ticketmaster and independent London venue programmes into a local SQLite database, flags tricky bills for review, tracks date and venue changes without treating missing feed items as cancellations, and publishes a static snapshot to Cloudflare.

Giggity — Data Pipeline & Ingestion Architecture

Giggity solves the grassroots discovery gap by consolidating commercial feeds and independent London venue programmes into a local SQLite repository with balance accounting and change tracking.

Discovery v2 + JSON-LD + HTML Scraping
Balance Accounting
Strict: fetched = accepted + rejected
Fingerprinting
SHA-256 (Title, Date, Time, Venue, Price)
Storage & Edge
SQLite (var/events.sqlite3) · Cloudflare KV

Archival radiance composite of China and East Asian megalopolises. Corrected for lunar irradiance, stray light, atmospheric scattering, and ephemeris orbital variation.
