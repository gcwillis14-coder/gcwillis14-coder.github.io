About

George Willis

I’m a data scientist at Cisco in London, working on internet performance. I try to learn new methods and investigate what the open data show.

My research background is in spatial networks and human movement. I’ve worked with taxi trips, satellite imagery and links between websites to study how places change and connect.

My personal projects usually start with something I’m interested in or something I’d find useful. Giggity grew out of hearing about London gigs too late and wanting one place to check venue calendars without losing track of what had changed. TransitFlow was built to teach a friend’s child a way to explore metro networks through maps and simulation.

I enjoy finding the data, working out its limitations and building something I can explore. This site collects that work, including ongoing projects, published research and a few older analyses.
