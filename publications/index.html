2022

Using the web to predict regional trade flows: data extraction, modelling, and validation

Trade between regions is difficult to observe directly. This paper investigates whether links between websites can help fill that gap.

We combined hyperlink networks with economic data and used predictive modelling to test whether online connections contained useful information about regional trade. The work brought together data extraction, network analysis and validation on later periods. With Emmanouil Tranos and André Carrascal-Incera.

Tranos, E., Carrascal-Incera, A. & Willis, G. (2022). Annals of the American Association of Geographers, 113(1), 168-189.

2021

Using "Big Data" to understand the impacts of Uber on taxis in New York City

This paper grew out of my undergraduate dissertation, examining Uber’s growth alongside changes in New York’s yellow taxi market.

The analysis combined taxi trip records, contextual information and time-series methods to investigate changing demand. With Emmanouil Tranos.
